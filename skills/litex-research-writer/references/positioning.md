# Litex Positioning

## Stable Scientific Core And Audience Overlays

Preserve the four-layer scientific invariant in `SKILL.md` in every full
positioning argument: scientific object → design hypothesis → measurable
result variables → potential societal impact. Litex itself is the scientific
problem. Downstream mathematics, AI, compiler, assurance, industrial, and
policy benefits motivate or test that problem; they do not replace it.

Change the foreground for the audience without changing the underlying claim:

- For a scientific paper such as Nature Computational Science, lead with
  Litex's scientific object, falsifiable design hypothesis, measurements,
  comparison, and boundary conditions. Treat downstream mathematical tasks as
  experiments or pressure tests. Do not introduce unmeasured AI benefits as
  current results.
- For a Seed application, lead with mathematical capacity, public formal data,
  reusable infrastructure, and what funding makes possible. Retain the
  scientific core so that Litex is not presented as only a software delivery
  project.
- For mathematicians, lead with why formal languages and AI for mathematics
  matter to mathematical construction, communication, and checking.
- For AI researchers, lead with machine-checkable feedback, formal data,
  repair traces, and assurance, while distinguishing current evidence from
  future agent studies.
- For industry, lead with the scarcity and value of trustworthy reasoning and
  sustainable applications without turning speculative markets into research
  results.
- For policy audiences, lead with foundational capability, scientific
  infrastructure, talent, and international compatibility without replacing
  scientific evidence with sovereignty claims.

Never let an audience overlay flow backward and redefine the primary question
of a scientific manuscript.

## For Proof Assistant Researchers

Litex is a complementary interface experiment: fact-oriented, readable, local proof repair, explicit proof debt, and benchmarkable failure categories.

Avoid claiming superiority over Lean. Instead ask whether a smaller checked-math interface can make some classes of formal data cheaper to produce.

## For AI-for-Math Researchers

Emphasize machine-checkable feedback, repair traces, benchmark slices, and formal data production.

Good claim: Litex exposes local mathematical failures that agents can repair.
Bad claim: Litex solves autoformalization.

## For Funders

Emphasize infrastructure, public corpus, benchmark, web editor, documentation, and soundness/scope work.

The funding story is that money turns a working artifact into reusable public infrastructure.

## For Educators

Emphasize readable checked mathematics, student proof practice, explicit facts, and fast feedback.
